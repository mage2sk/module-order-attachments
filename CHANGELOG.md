## [1.1.8] - 2026-10-06

### Fixed
- Guest orders: the attachment Download link and image thumbnails on the Orders and Returns order view now work for the guest who looked up the order, instead of sending the visitor to the home page. Access is checked against the store's guest order lookup, so other visitors still cannot open the files.
